# Public Samachar — Google Play Store Publishing Assets

This directory contains the release build files, logo graphics, feature banners, and screenshots compiled for submission to the Google Play Console.

## Store Listing Text Metadata

* **App Name:** My Public Samachara
* **Short Description:** Kannada Local News & Video Reporting App. Publish news instantly as a reporter!
* **Full Description:**
  My Public Samachar is a local news reporting platform designed for verified news reporters and local correspondents. Capture videos, write headlines, select locations, and publish local news instantly to the public feed.
  
  Key Features:
  - Direct Video Upload: Upload news videos directly from your phone camera or gallery.
  - Location Tagging: Tag news stories with specific local regions and municipalities.
  - Video Editor integration.
  - Dark-mode, glassmorphic feed for reading and viewing stories.
